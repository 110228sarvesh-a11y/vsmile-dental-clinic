MEET YOUR DENTIST

Dr. Abhirami Srikanth

M.D.S., FIOO · Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon · Oral Oncologist

Dr. Abhirami Srikanth completed her BDS from Bapuji Dental College and Hospital, Davangere in 2019 and her MDS in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery from SRM Institute of Science and Technology in 2023.

Following this, she pursued a fellowship in Oral Oncology at Sri Ramachandra Institute of Higher Education and Research.

Her interests include Oral Cancer, benign pathologies and facial trauma.

She has published various research articles in both indexed national and international journals and has also published a book on Treatment Options in Head and Neck Cancer.

Currently a Senior Lecturer at Madha Dental College and Hospital, she has academic experience of over two years.
